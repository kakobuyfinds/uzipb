03 / From discovery to decision

Make the link useful.

What does a spreadsheet help you do?

A CNShopper spreadsheet is a starting point for product research: a way to organise finds and their source links. It cannot confirm current stock, fit or seller reliability. Keep the original listing and selected option beside every item you save.

01 — IDENTIFY

Keep the source.

CNShopper product links are easier to check when you retain the underlying marketplace address. Taobao product link checks, Weidian seller checks and 1688 listing checks should identify the same item and variant.

  • Record the original seller listing and item ID.
  • Confirm colourway and size selection.
  • Recheck a broken spreadsheet link at its source.
02 — COMPARE

Measure with context.

Use a CNShopper size guide as a prompt to read the seller's chart. Compare centimetres with something you already wear. A hoodie fit comparison should include chest width, body length and sleeve shape.

  • Check whether measurements are flat or around the body.
  • Compare T-shirt chest measurements and length.
  • For pants waist measurements, include rise and inseam.
03 — REVIEW

Inspect your order.

CNShopper QC photos can help reveal visible differences before dispatch. Existing photos from other orders are references; a warehouse photo review should also cover the item bought for you.

  • Match labels, quantity and selected options.
  • Request missing angles where the service permits.
  • Check current return terms before approving shipment.

04 / The buying notebook

Two short reads.
Better-informed choices.

Practical notes for comparing CNShopper finds without treating every picture as a recommendation.

Fit & materials / Reading note

Make a shortlist that fits your wardrobe.

Start with a gap you want to fill: a light layer, everyday sneakers or an accessory that works with several outfits. A clothing spreadsheet guide is most useful when it helps you compare a few relevant options instead of saving every image.

Write down the measurements of a comfortable garment. Use that reference for oversized hoodie sizing, jacket sleeve length and T-shirt length comparisons. Check whether the seller describes fabric composition and provides clear care information.

  • For a matching set size check, measure both pieces.
  • For jersey sizing advice, establish whether you want a close or relaxed fit.
  • For accessory dimensions, compare against a real item or ruler.

A recognisable logo does not establish origin or quality. If authenticity matters to your purchase, use an authorised retail channel and retain the relevant purchase documentation.

Photos & evidence / Reading note

Read QC photos as evidence, not a promise.

A QC photo checklist starts with identification: is the pictured item from the exact listing and selected option? Compare the front, back, size label and any included pieces. Lighting can change apparent colour, so avoid drawing a conclusion from one photograph.

Seller photos versus QC photos is a comparison of presentation and observation. Neither can prove fibre content, comfort or durability. Ask for a measurement photo when fit is uncertain, and keep a record of the answer.

  • Inspect stitching and seam alignment without assuming hidden construction.
  • For a sneaker colourway comparison, match panels and labels to the selected option.
  • Use a return policy check before approving an item with unresolved issues.

Reference QC from another buyer does not replace inspection of your own order. Confirm the service's current inspection scope, request process and return window directly.

The product price is only one part of the budget.

Use a CNShopper shipping guide to prepare questions, then confirm the current quote with the service handling the parcel. International shipping estimates depend on destination, parcel measurements, carrier rules and the chosen service.

05 / Straight answers

Before you
follow a find.

Quick answers about links, fit, photos and the purpose of this page.

What is a CNShopper spreadsheet?

A CNShopper spreadsheet is a curated collection of product references used to research items for ordering through CNShopper. A list may be a sheet or a website. This page is an independent guide; its browsing links lead to cafans.com.

Can I place a CNShopper order on this page?

No. This page does not connect to a CNShopper account or process orders. Category links open the named cafans.com categories; other navigation opens the cafans.com homepage.

Do QC photos prove an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible details such as colour, stitching and measurements. They do not certify authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.

How should I choose a clothing or shoe size?

Compare the exact listing's measurements with a garment that fits you. For shoes, compare foot length with the model-specific size chart and confirm the measurement method. Letter sizes and regional labels alone are not enough.

Does a listed product price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international freight and any applicable taxes or duties before paying. Confirm current charges with the service handling the order.

Can a Taobao, Weidian or 1688 link always be purchased?

No. A product reference does not guarantee purchasing support or availability. Check the original listing, selected option and the purchasing service's current restrictions before ordering.

Are the brand images proof of cafans.com stock?

No. Images are editorial style references. They do not confirm that the pictured model is available on cafans.com, establish authenticity of another listing or imply a brand partnership.
